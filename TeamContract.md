# Team Contract
---
## Purpose of this Contract

This contract sets out shared expectations and commitments for how our team will collaborate during the course. It is designed to promote accountability, professionalism, and mutual support as we complete individual preparation, readiness assurance tests, in-class activities, and our course project.

---
## Team Norms and Expectations

### Communication

* Instagram and Discord for our modes of communication with primary usage being on Discord for all project related questions, collaboration, and co

* Each teammate agrees to respond to messages at the latest they are able to, ideally within 12-24 hours.

* Each teammate will let the team if they are unable to:
- Attend lecture
- Attend tutorial
- Unable to meet a deadline or have difficulty with assigned work

---
## AI Usage

* As a team we have agreed on the use of generative AI coding tools such as but not limited too Claude Code, Codex, Antigravity, and etc...
- We will only commit AI generated code on the condition that you understand the code at a structural level where you can explain how it exists within the Clean Architecture system and can explain the use and function of every class
- Generated code will follow Clean Architecture and SOLID principles (to the best degree we can manage)
- Generated code should not be all dumped in one PR but reasonably commited in chunks with functional changes to the codebase

---

## Decision Making

* Decisions will be made by consensus of all team members with majority vote breaking ties

---
## Conflict resolution

* How will your team resolve conflicts? 
- Talk it out.

---

## Accountability

* Reliability and accountability
- Do not be afraid to seek help and ask questions.
- If you are experiencing difficulty communicate it with the team.
